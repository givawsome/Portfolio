03 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

Hi, I’mRajneesh.

A visual designer with a photographer’s eye and a curiosity for what comes next.

I began in photography and photo editing in 2017. My practice grew through graphic design, running a customized gifting business, and creating imagery for e-commerce.

Today, I work in pharmaceutical e-commerce, bringing together product photography, creative design and UI work. Alongside my role, I take on select freelance projects.

See more about meShow less⌄

Photography first. A broader practice since.

My journey began at Apex Studio in Jaipur in 2017, photographing events and refining images in Photoshop and Lightroom. At Wow Graphics in Jodhpur, I brought that experience into customized products, print artwork, school photography and catalog design.

From 2021 to 2024, I ran Rudra Customized Gifting. Working directly with customers meant taking ideas from the first conversation through artwork, photography, listings and production—including selling through Amazon, Flipkart and Meesho.

From social stories to e-commerce.

At DKM Diagnostics, I created social content and managed Instagram, Facebook and X. Since September 2025, my role at Asle Pharmaceuticals has brought together product photography, retouching, banners, catalogs and Figma UI design, wireframing and website testing.

My CV records more than 22,689 product images prepared for e-commerce and creative work across around eight to nine website projects. That experience connects the small details of an image with the wider experience of a page.

What I bring to a freelance project.

I can help with product imagery, visual identity, campaign and social creatives, and website visuals. AI-assisted concepts and character work sit alongside my photography and design practice, with each approach clearly identified in the galleries.

I’m based in Jaipur and take on select freelance projects alongside my current role. A clear brief, thoughtful visual direction and careful finishing guide how I work.
